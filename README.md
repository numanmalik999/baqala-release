## ✨ System Features

- **ZATCA Phase 2 Fatoora Certified:** Cryptographic invoice hashing, ECDSA secp256k1 signing, and TLV QR codes.
- **Dual Business Mode:** Supermarket & Retail POS / Restaurant & Dine-In floor plans & KDS.
- **Offline SQLite Engine:** Full local database operations with peer LAN syncing and cloud reconciliation.
- **Multi-Branch Management:** Unified inventory transfers, central catalog, and role-based permissions.
- **Hardware Integration:** Thermal receipt printers (ESC/POS), barcode label printers, scales, and cash drawers.
